Green line: greenness (NDVI). Red line: daytime ground temperature. April 2026 had the lowest greenness and one of the hottest months, during PAGASA's April dry spell.

Data

  • Landsat HLS L30 vegetation indices (EVI, NBR, NDMI, NDWI), 30 m, Jan 2025 – Sep 2026
  • MODIS MOD13Q1 NDVI (250 m) and MOD11A2 land temperature (1 km)
  • ALOS AW3D30 elevation; 2020 regional land cover (province outlines)
  • Earthquake: M7.8, 8 Jun 2026, 32 km SSW of Maasim (PHIVOLCS; AHA Centre report)

Limits

  • The optical data stop at about 124.93°E, so western Sarangani (Maasim, Kiamba, Maitum) has no landslide check.
  • No elevation data south of 5.9°N in the source used, so Glan, Jose Abad Santos and the islands were not checked for slides.
  • Heavy rain or land clearing can look like a landslide. Treat patches as places to check, not confirmed slides.
  • These layers show surface change, not ground uplift. That needs NISAR interferometry (GUNW) spanning 8 June.
